    Two numbers, two questions. Registry coverage asks whether a block, entity type or item has a behaviour implementation at all — the question most server trackers put on their front page. Method coverage asks the stricter question of how much of each vanilla class's surface is matched, so it is always the lower figure. Neither is a behaviour guarantee.

    How to read this. A class counts as complete when every method the decompile lists has a plausibly-named counterpart somewhere in the workspace. Matching is by name, so a method counted as missing may in fact be implemented under a different name or inlined into its caller — treat the missing counts as leads to check, not as a defect list. Coverage here is also about surface, not behaviour: a matched method can still behave differently from vanilla.
